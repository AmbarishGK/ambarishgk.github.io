MoRAL: Sensor-Grounded BEV Reasoning for Compact VLMs toward Edge-Oriented Autonomous Driving

Ambarish Govindarajulu Kaliamurthi, Kaikai Liu · IEEE IMC 2026, Fukuoka, Japan (presented) · arXiv:2608.02449

NVIDIA Cosmos Reason 2, trained in two stages: first it learns to read a bird's-eye view we render from LiDAR and radar, then it learns driving reasoning (what is approaching, whether to brake). Presented at IEEE IMC 2026 in Fukuoka.
